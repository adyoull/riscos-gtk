@NAME@ @VERSION@ - where its source is

This program is built with riscos-gtk (GTK 3 for RISC OS), @RELEASE@,
from riscos-gtk commit @COMMIT@.

It contains, statically linked, GTK, GLib, pango, cairo, gdk-pixbuf, ATK,
fribidi and their libraries. These are under the GNU Lesser General
Public License version 2.1 (LicenceLGPL), and other free licences listed
in riscos-gtk's LICENCES.md.

You may change those libraries and relink this program with your
changes. The complete corresponding source, which is everything needed
to rebuild this program, is the riscos-gtk source archive published
with this release (riscos-gtk-<release>-src.tar). It contains:
- riscos-gtk at the commit above: the RISC OS backend, the patches to
  each library, and this program's RISC OS code and build scripts;
- the upstream source of every library and of the program itself.

docs/BUILDING.md in the archive says how to rebuild. The source is at:

  @SOURCE_URL@

If you received this program without the source and can't find it
there, ask the person who gave it to you, or the maintainer (Andrew

release date.
